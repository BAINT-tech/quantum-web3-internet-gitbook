## Future Vision (2028-2030 and Beyond)

Beyond the initial mainnet launch, our long-term vision extends to:

*   **Quantum Internet Integration:** Exploring deeper integration with emerging quantum internet infrastructure for truly secure and private communication channels.
*   **Quantum-Enhanced Consensus:** Researching and implementing novel quantum-enhanced consensus mechanisms for improved efficiency and security.
*   **Broader dApp Ecosystem:** Fostering a rich ecosystem of quantum-secure dApps across various sectors, including DeFi, gaming, supply chain, and healthcare.
*   **Education and Advocacy:** Continuing to educate the public and industry about the importance of quantum security in Web3 and advocating for the adoption of quantum-resistant standards.
*   **Open-Source Contributions:** Maintaining our commitment to open-source development, contributing to the broader quantum and blockchain communities.

This roadmap is a living document that will evolve as we achieve our milestones and adapt to the dynamic landscape of quantum computing and blockchain technology. We invite our community to join us on this exciting journey as we build the foundations for the QUANTUM•WEB3•INTERNET.
